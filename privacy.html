Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

OutThere respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we may use it, and the choices you have when you visit our website or contact us.

Information We Collect

We do not currently require user accounts, purchases, or sign-up forms on this website. We may receive personal information only when you choose to provide it, such as when you contact us through a linked social media page or another communication channel.

Like most websites, our hosting provider and browser-based services may automatically process limited technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and the date and time of your visit.

How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • respond to messages or questions you send us;
  • maintain, protect, and improve the website;
  • understand general website performance and visitor activity;
  • prevent misuse, spam, fraud, or technical issues.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use cookies or similar technologies if enabled by our hosting, analytics, or embedded third-party services. You can usually adjust your browser settings to block or delete cookies, although some website features may not work as expected.

Third-Party Services

Our website may include links, fonts, images, social media pages, or other services provided by third parties. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites or services.

Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only when needed to operate the website, respond to your requests, comply with legal obligations, protect our rights, or prevent misuse of the website.

Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Your Choices

You may ask us to review, correct, or delete personal information that you have provided to us. You can also choose not to provide personal information and can adjust cookie settings through your browser.

Children's Privacy

OutThere is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, please contact us through the official OutThere social media page linked in the footer of this website.
